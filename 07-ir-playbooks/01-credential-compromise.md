# IR Playbook - Credential Compromise

## Trigger

Suspicious authentication or credential-access detection.

## Steps

1. Validate alert.
2. Identify affected account.
3. Review authentication timeline.
4. Review source hosts/IPs.
5. Check privilege level.
6. Reset credentials according to organizational procedure.
7. Revoke active sessions where appropriate.
8. Search for lateral movement.
9. Preserve evidence.
10. Document closure.

## Evidence

Authentication logs, endpoint logs, process telemetry and identity records.
