# IR Playbook - Lateral Movement

## Trigger

SMB, remote service, RDP or administrative-share detection.

## Steps

1. Validate source and destination.
2. Identify account used.
3. Build timeline.
4. Review process creation.
5. Review authentication events.
6. Isolate affected systems according to IR policy.
7. Search for additional destinations.
8. Preserve evidence.
9. Remove persistence if confirmed.
10. Document recovery.
